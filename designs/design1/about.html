Our Story

Architecture in Motion

For over a decade, Poperty has curated the world’s most distinctive modernist residences, pairing architectural pedigree with seamless client execution.

We believe space shapes consciousness and living.

Founded by architectural historians and luxury real estate advisors, Poperty was built to bridge the gap between museum-grade architectural design and residential living.

Every property in our private registry is evaluated on architectural merit, natural illumination, materiality, and enduring investment value.
